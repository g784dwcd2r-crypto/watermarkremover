## What the service does and does not promise

Image reconstruction is estimation. The software fills a selected region with
plausible content derived from its surroundings; it does not recover what was
underneath. Results on complex textures, large removals and fine structure vary,
and some selections will not produce a usable result.

Similarly, a reconstructed timelapse is a plausible sequence, not a record. No
claim is made that it reflects how the artwork was actually produced.

The service is provided as-is, without warranty of fitness for a particular
purpose.
